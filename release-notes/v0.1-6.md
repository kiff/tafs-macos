## English

### tafs for Mac (macOS 12) 0.1-6

**This is a beta: some things may not work as they should yet. Updates follow.**

**What it does:** tafs on your Mac: your tafs, what needs you, and Coach. With a USB security key, this Mac can now approve too: you confirm each approval on the key with its PIN. Connecting an app is still approved on your phone.

**In this release:**
- Alerts with tafs closed: when a taf needs you, this Mac tells you even when tafs isn't open. Turn them on in Needs you or in Account › Alerts, and choose there what alerts you.
- An alert opens Needs you, or your brief for a brief's alert. An alert for a call answered elsewhere goes away by itself.
- Sign out turns this Mac's alerts off first. If it can't, it asks you before signing out.
- Try tafs with sample data: its held calls alert you too, as on the iPad.
- Coach's chat shows when Coach asks to drive your phone, and what it did there.
- A held call in the sidebar opens its taf's page.

**What it needs:** a Mac on macOS 12 (Monterey), Intel or Apple silicon, and the phone you use with tafs, to pair and approve. To approve on this Mac, a USB security key with a PIN. This app is for Macs that stay on macOS 12; for newer macOS, get tafs at https://kiff.dev/tafs/download.

**Install:** Open the disk image and drag tafs to Applications. It's signed by Kiff Agents OU and notarized by Apple.

**Check the file:** In Terminal: `shasum -a 256 tafs-0.1-6.dmg` and compare the result with the SHA-256 above. If they differ, delete the file and don't open it.
