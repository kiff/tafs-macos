## English

### tafs for Mac (macOS 12) 0.1-7

**This is a beta: some things may not work as they should yet. Updates follow.**

**What it does:** tafs on your Mac: your tafs, what needs you, and Coach. With a USB security key, this Mac can now approve too: you confirm each approval on the key with its PIN. Connecting an app is still approved on your phone.

**In this release:**
- Needs you: once you answer a held call, its paused run goes on at once. If the run had already ended, Needs you says so.
- A wide window: every page is one column in the middle, no wider than reads well, instead of sitting against the left. In Account, where your approvals are signed now sits under Your account, and How tafs works is one entry, under Legal.
- “Allow this from now on”: this Mac approves a change to a Card with your security key only if it can read every term in it. A term it doesn't know is never approved unseen.

**What it needs:** a Mac on macOS 12 (Monterey), Intel or Apple silicon, and the phone you use with tafs, to pair and approve. To approve on this Mac, a USB security key with a PIN. This app is for Macs that stay on macOS 12; for newer macOS, get tafs at https://kiff.dev/tafs/download.

**Install:** Open the disk image and drag tafs to Applications. It's signed by Kiff Agents OU and notarized by Apple.

**Check the file:** In Terminal: `shasum -a 256 tafs-0.1-7.dmg` and compare the result with the SHA-256 above. If they differ, delete the file and don't open it.
