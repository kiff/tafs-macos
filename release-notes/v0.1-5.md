## English

### tafs for Mac (macOS 12) 0.1-5

**This is a beta: some things may not work as they should yet. Updates follow.**

**What it does:** tafs on your Mac: your tafs, what needs you, and Coach. With a USB security key, this Mac can now approve too: you confirm each approval on the key with its PIN. Connecting an app is still approved on your phone.

**In this release:**
- Approve on a USB security key: Approve once, Extra room, Allow this from now on, Did this happen?, a taf's Card, sharing a taf, and a door's keys.
- On a Mac with Touch ID: change groups, and end an app's access.
- Plan & usage: who bills your plan, Raise the limit (confirmed each time, in your account's currency), and where to buy on a computer.
- Try tafs with sample data, in your language, before you connect anything. Sign in inside the app, with the new first steps.
- Coach: an approval shows what it asks in full, with what KIFF counts; a drafted door; the taf taking shape; your watch's conversations.
- A taf's chat and page as on the iPad: runs and refusals in place, held calls, Connect again, and a run that opens from its line.
- The Record: a receipt's facts, the page before a Chrome step, and Not sent with Send again.
- Install a version of a taf on this Mac, and add WhatsApp's door.
- Account › Chrome: each Chrome says what it's used for, and Unpair says which connections it ends.

**What it needs:** a Mac on macOS 12 (Monterey), Intel or Apple silicon, and the phone you use with tafs, to pair and approve. To approve on this Mac, a USB security key with a PIN. This app is for Macs that stay on macOS 12; for newer macOS, get tafs at https://kiff.dev/tafs/download.

**Install:** Open the disk image and drag tafs to Applications. It's signed by Kiff Agents OU and notarized by Apple.

**Check the file:** In Terminal: `shasum -a 256 tafs-0.1-5.dmg` and compare the result with the SHA-256 above. If they differ, delete the file and don't open it.
